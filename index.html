House rules — from the shop sheets

1

Strawberry drinks: smash 1 medium strawberry (or ½ of a big one) in the bottom of the cup for texture

2

Fruit-flavored milk teas: now 75% sugar (handwritten correction on the sheets — was 100%)

3

Milkshake base: milkshake cup, whip cream by default — 1 cup ice cream + big cup full of ice. Sugar: base sheet says 100%, the Korea sheet's handwritten note says 75%

4

Cups: Matcha Specialty & Taste of Korea drinks use milkshake cups (except Matcha Love, Ube Mood, Korean Coffee); lattes with foam go in a small cup with lids

Ube Milk Tea

50% sugar

  1. 16 oz cup (handwritten: small cup), fill with ice
  2. In a measuring cup: 250 ml milk + 50% sugar + ube paste drizzle for 3 seconds (like ube ice cream), froth with the frother
  3. Pour into the cup — should fill to about 1 inch from the rim
  4. Foam: 50 ml heavy cream + 25% sugar + very tiny splash of milk, froth until it doubles in size and is creamy
  5. Pour the foam on top and serve
